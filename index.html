DUNGEON GENERATOR

Four ways to carve a floor plan out of solid rock, each with a different feel: architecture, erosion, wandering and sprawl. Every one of them is seeded, so a level you like is a number you can keep — and every one is checked for connectivity before you see it, because a dungeon with an unreachable treasure room is a bug report.
